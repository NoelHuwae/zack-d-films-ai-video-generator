## 💡 Tips for Better Videos

**Pick topics with a built-in question.** "Why do we get goosebumps?" beats "Goosebumps explained." The first one makes someone need the answer.

**Keep it short.** The format works best when it's tight. Long videos lose the hook.

**Reuse your characters.** Once you have a character sheet you like, keep using it across videos. It builds recognition and saves time.

**Check the script before the animation.** Scripts are quick to regenerate; full videos are not. Read the script, adjust the angle if needed, then let it render.

**Batch your topics.** Write down ten ideas in one sitting. Then you always have something ready to make.
